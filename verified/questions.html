Questions and information needed

What is known, what is not, and how to find out. Decisions that depend on an open question should wait for the answer.

QuestionStatusWhat the official sources showHow to resolve
What dates is the trip?UnansweredNot given. The Venetian series runs Nov 16 to Dec 23, 2026; the Wynn WPT runs Nov 27 to Dec 21, 2026 (both official).Give the dates. The day schedule and hotel pages can then be limited to those nights.
Can you play from 8 AM all day?Answered: not with official dataNo printed start is before 9:10 AM in the Venetian series (a satellite). Most flights start at 10:10 or 11:10 AM. No finish times are printed, so a full day cannot be confirmed.Ask the Venetian and Wynn poker rooms for typical finish times and whether late registration allows a later entry.
Are the Venetian events single-entry?Partly answered24 of 83 flights are on structure sheets that were read; none of those sheets states a re-entry rule. The other 59 flights are not checked.Read the remaining sheets (the structure sheet links are on the single-entry page) or ask the Venetian poker room.
Are the Wynn WPT events single-entry?Partly answered4 Nov 27 rows are re-entry-verified. The other 112 buy-in rows are not verified.Read each event's structure sheet in the official structures PDF.
What does 'prize pool' mean here?AnsweredThe site uses the printed guarantee. The actual paid prize pool depends on the field, which the rooms do not print.Compare results PDFs after events run.
What are the entry requirements?Partly answeredWynn WPT schedule: players must be at least 21. Venetian structure sheet for #26: 21+ with a Venetian Rewards card.Read the structure sheet for each event you plan to enter.
What is the true nightly hotel cost?UnansweredResort fees are verified for three hotels ($55 plus tax). No room rate or tax was verified, so the total is blank.Enter the rate from the official booking page for your dates into the calculator on the Hotels page.
What is the Clark County tax rate?UnansweredThe official page returned a 404. Third-party sites say 13.38%. Not used.Confirm with Clark County or the LVCVA and enter it in the calculator.
How far is each hotel from each poker room on foot?UnansweredNo official source gives walking times. Poker-room locations inside the resorts are not confirmed.Measure the route on a map tool, record the date and source, and add it to the data.
Which hotels should be compared?Partly answeredThree hotels are included: The Venetian, Wynn Las Vegas and Resorts World Las Vegas. Other Strip hotels were not verified (flag F13).Decide which other hotels to verify (Suggestions S3).
How should 'best hotel' be judged?UnansweredNo quality measure is defined or verified.Define the measure (for example a star rating or review score) and name the source.
Should satellites and women-only or 50+ events be included?UnansweredThey are listed. Satellites print seats, and some events have an eligibility rule (for example LIPS Ladies, Seniors 50+).Decide whether to include them in the rankings.
Should Wynn daily tournaments be included?UnansweredThe official poster is undated and says the daily schedule is suspended during some series.Ask the Wynn poker room whether daily events run on your dates.
What is the budget?UnansweredNot given.Provide a budget so the plan can be capped.
