# Hotels Within Walking Distance of Las Vegas Strip Poker Tournaments

**Criterion:** Reasonable walking distance = within 30 minutes on foot (~1.5 miles) of a tournament venue. Primary venues: The Venetian Poker Room (3355 S Las Vegas Blvd) and Wynn/Encore Poker Room (3131 S Las Vegas Blvd), which are one pedestrian bridge apart (~0.5 mile).

**True cost per night formula:** (Room rate + nightly resort fee) × (1 + tax rate) + nightly parking
- Clark County room tax: 13.38% — [Venetian Hotel Terms & Conditions (official)](https://www.venetianlasvegas.com/legal/hotel-terms-and-conditions.html)
- Wynn/Encore stay terms confirm ~$62.36 = $55 fee + 13.38% tax — [Wynn review citing official stay terms](https://hoteltiplasvegas.com/wynn-las-vegas-review/) (third-party summary of Wynn's official stay terms)

## Notes

- Walk times/distances above are approximate, derived from published street addresses (official hotel sites). No resort publishes official walking times; treat as estimates and verify with walking directions before booking. All distances are well within a 30-minute walk except Resorts World from the Venetian (borderline ~30 min) — Resorts World is minutes from Wynn.
- Room rates for November–December 2026 are NOT published yet (hotels typically open booking ~330 days ahead; Venetian poker suite rates are only provided max 150 days out). Leave blank until confirmed — do not infer rates.
- Venetian poker suite rates (official): start as low as $119 (Venetian) / $209 (Palazzo) per night, require 6 hours of active live cash play OR a $300+ tournament buy-in each night stayed, code EPOKR25, max 150 days out, subject to availability. A player entering $300+ single-entry tournaments can qualify; $200 events do not meet the $300+ buy-in requirement.
- The Venetian charges registered hotel guests $20/day self-parking; Wynn/Encore self-parking is free for registered guests (per Wynn stay terms summary — flag for reconfirmation).
- Incidentals hold: ~$150/night credit-card authorization at Wynn; Venetian $150/night for non-Signature suites (per official terms) — not part of nightly cost, but budget for it.
- Example (illustrative only, NOT a quote): if a room rate were $200/night at The Venetian: ($200 + $55) × 1.1338 + $20 = ~$309.12/night true cost. At Wynn (no parking): ($200 + $55) × 1.1338 + $0 = ~$289.12/night.
