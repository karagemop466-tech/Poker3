How true cost per night is calculated

Formula: True cost per night = (Room rate + nightly resort fee) × (1 + tax rate) + nightly parking
  • Room tax: 13.38% Clark County room tax — Venetian Hotel Terms & Conditions (official)
  • Resort fee: $55 + tax per night at The Venetian and at Wynn/Encore (official sources below); with 13.38% tax the fee alone is about $62.36/night.
  • Parking: The Venetian $20/day self-parking for registered hotel guests (official); Wynn/Encore free self-parking for registered guests (per Wynn stay terms — flagged for reconfirmation).
  • Room rates for Nov–Dec 2026: not yet published — left blank and flagged (see below). No rates are inferred.
Illustrative example only — NOT a quote: if a room rate were $200/night: The Venetian = ($200 + $55) × 1.1338 + $20 ≈ $309.12/night; Wynn = ($200 + $55) × 1.1338 + $0 ≈ $289.12/night. Your actual cost depends on the room rate at booking.

Poker player hotel rate (Venetian — official, conditional)

The Venetian offers poker suite rates starting as low as $119/night (Venetian) / $209/night (Palazzo), subject to availability, with these conditions (official):

  • Book by phone (866.255.8936) with code EPOKR25; rates provided max 150 days out; future rates beyond that window are not provided.
  • Require 6 hours of active live cash play OR a buy-in to a $300+ tournament each night stayed. $200 single-entry tournaments do not meet the $300+ buy-in requirement — a player on the $200 events would need cash play or $300+ tournaments to keep the rate.
  • $55 resort fee per night still applies; upgrades charged separately.
