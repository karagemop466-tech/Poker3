## Discrepancies and irregularities (for manual review; no value changed)

- **D1 — MGM Grand start times differ between the official HTML page and the official weekly PDF.** The HTML page
  says 11:00am / 7:00pm / 11:00pm. The PDF says 11:10am / 7:10pm / 11:10pm. The repo follows the PDF. A venue
  reader should confirm which is current before booking.
- **D2 — Venetian event numbers are reused for different events** in the same official PDF (e.g., #47 is listed for the
  1A and 1B title events and a Day 2 restart; #56 for five different 1A–1E and restart rows; #19 for Nov 29, Nov 30,
  Dec 1 and a Dec 2 restart). Rows must be keyed by date and time, not by event number alone.
- **D3 — Caesars schedule is published only in a marketing article** (dated July 17, 2026, on the Caesars site),
  with no structure sheet and no entry terms. The times are therefore sourced to an article, not a schedule.
- **D4 — Caesars Palace daily events include a $50 add-on at the first break** (per the same article). This is outside
  the single-entry rule and is excluded from the main list.
- **D5 — Venetian DSE IV, 2026-11-19 to 11-21 (Events 3–5, 10:10 AM)** are titled "Race to finish before the Race"; the
  schedule PDF lists them under the same title on each day. Not a discrepancy with the repo, but the format is unusual
  and its entry rules need the written structure sheet.
