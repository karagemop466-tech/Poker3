## Terms the room does print (not entry rules)

- Event titles on the schedule carry "**DIRECT ENTRY INTO EVENT #47**" (satellite awards), "**RAKE DISCOUNT FOR ENTRIES BEFORE** …" and "**RACEDAY STACK … RACE TO FINISH**". These describe prizes and discounts. They do not say how many times a player may enter.
- Multi-flight events (e.g. "1A/1B/1C/1D") are already excluded from the single-entry list on the site for the same reason as before; their flight structure does not establish an entry cap.
