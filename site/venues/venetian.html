Venue notes

Entry rule read

Single entry, from the sheet itself

18 of the 37 sheets in this series print one buy-in, one start flight, one starting-chip figure and no re-entry, rebuy or add-on clause, and only those rows are the sheet-screened candidates. The other 19 do print an extra-entry clause: 13 publish separate starting flights, 3 are satellites, 2 are restricted LIPS formats and 1 is a multi-buy-in bounty. That wording is recorded as “single entry on the sheet” with the excerpt attached; the sheet never uses the words “single entry”, and “1Day” on its own is still only a format, not an entry rule.

Estimates, clearly labelled

Finish times are not published

No venue prints a finish time. The planner estimates a session from the printed late-registration cut-off and flags a pair as an impossible sequence when the second start falls inside the estimated session plus a 30-minute transfer. Every such flag is an estimate.

Rate is a dated log

Several dated totals, and a floor

The poker-suite page lists starting rates of $119 and $209 across Venetian/Palazzo without mapping each to a tower; it is conditional on six hours of cash play or a $300+ tournament buy-in per night. The resort fee is $55 plus tax and the hotel terms print 13.38% Clark County room tax on the daily rate. The official booking engine printed $1,245.48 for 17-19 Nov (read 16 Nov), $1,015.89 for the same two nights (read 17 Nov), $4,010.55 for 23-30 Nov at the Venetian and $3,823.46 for the same seven nights at the Palazzo (both read 17 Nov). Compare like-for-like by suite name, party size and read time: the same query returned two different totals twenty minutes apart (F78). No figure from a failed 2026-11-19 render was recorded.
