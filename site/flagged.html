How unverified values are shown

The convention used everywhere on this site.

Blank

“Not published / unverified”

Used where no official source published the value. Examples: Horseshoe buy-ins, guarantees and level lengths; MGM Grand and ARIA hotel resort fees; the room-tax percentage at Wynn, Bellagio, Caesars Palace and Horseshoe, where each publish “plus applicable tax” without the number. Caesars Palace and Horseshoe resort fees were blank in an earlier pass and are now published from the properties’ own booking pages ($54.95 + $7.35 tax and $49.95 + $6.68 tax), which is how a blank is meant to close.

Labelled

Estimate, floor, or ratio

Used where a number is derived rather than printed: the estimated finish time on a tournament row, the true nightly cost computed from published components, and the guaranteed-pool ÷ fee ratio. Each is labelled where it appears, and the method is published on the same page.

Isolated

Extra-spend formats

Used for re-entry, rebuy, add-on, unlimited-rebuy and optional paid chip-boost formats. These rows are never presented as single-entry options, and they are never used to justify a ranking.

Not ranked

Source conflicts

Used where two official pages give different values for the same event, such as the Bellagio Saturday buy-in. The affected row keeps every field blank and is excluded from the ranking and the day plan.
