Decisions already taken, so they are not re-litigated

  • Cost and quality come before distance, but every hotel shown is within the 30-minute walking limit. A cheaper stay further out does not qualify.
  • Blank means blank. A value that could not be read from an official source is left empty and listed on the Flagged page, never filled with a plausible number.
  • Third-party material is never evidence. Walking estimates are labelled as estimates and carry a map link so a traveller can check them.
  • Nothing is ranked before its fee and guarantee are confirmed on an official sheet.
Built from official venue pages on 2026-11-16. A guarantee is what a venue publishes, not a forecast of a payout. Verify prices, terms and schedules with the room before you pay. Limitations and flagged items.
