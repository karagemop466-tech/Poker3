P1 · Research rules · recommended

Choose a strict entry policy

Option A (recommended): include only tournaments whose official event terms explicitly state one entry / freezeout and no rebuy, re-entry, add-on, or extra chips. Keep unknown rules out of “best.”
Option B: allow one entry per flight, but show each flight separately and label the additional entry opportunity.
Option C: permit re-entry events in a distinct category. This weakens the original single-entry constraint.

Tradeoff: strict criteria can produce an empty shortlist; that is more honest than treating silence as confirmation.

P1 · Hotel scoring · recommended

Do not invent a “best hotel” score

Option A (recommended): compare date-specific checkout totals first, then apply the user's quality threshold and a confirmed walking-time ceiling.
Option B: use a defined quality measure (e.g., official star classification) if one is available and sourced consistently; otherwise show official amenities and leave quality unranked.

Current official rates are conditional or missing exact dates; table all-in nightly totals remain blank pending a real quote. Distance should be measured from the actual guest-room/poker-room access points, not estimated from property addresses.

After the stay dates are fixed, add each official room's event calendar for those exact dates. Include only events within the user's 30-minute walking ceiling after route verification. Keep non-Strip venues in a separate tab so they cannot silently dilute access standards.

P2 · All-day planning

Add duration and conflict-aware scheduling

Capture late-registration cutoff, levels, break schedule, format, and actual historical duration where the venue publishes it. Render overlapping sessions as conflicts, not stacked entries. A day timeline can highlight the earliest verifiable start and the last realistic entry window.

Do not promise a continuous 8 AM playday unless a venue's date-specific official schedule supports it.

P3 · Research workflow

Use direct room confirmation for rules that web pages omit

Public official schedules often show the buy-in and guarantee but omit one-entry / re-entry restrictions. Add a dated contact-confirmation record for the event number, time, rule, and staff contact—then retain the official PDF or written venue response for audit.

Do not upgrade a candidate to eligible based on third-party calendars or customary poker-room practice.
