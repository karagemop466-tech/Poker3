Open questions

The decisions that are still outstanding before money is committed. Each one says what is unknown, what to ask, who to ask, and what on this site changes when the answer arrives.

Q1Is a second buy-in possible in the one-day Venetian events?

The one-day structure sheets are read in full and simply do not mention re-entry, a rebuy, an add-on or a paid chip boost. That is silence, not permission and not a refusal. The desk's actual policy for a player who is eliminated early is not published.

Ask: Call the Venetian poker room and ask what happens if you are eliminated before registration closes. Ask them to put it in writing for the event number.

Who to ask: The Venetian poker room. The official poker page lists the room and its hours.

What it affects: Affects every row in the 'Single entry not stated' bucket on the Tournaments page.

Q2How late does each event actually run?

Official sheets publish start time, blind levels, level length and the time registration closes. None of them publishes a finish time. A tournament can run hours past its registration cut-off, so this site cannot promise a precise end.

Ask: Ask each room for the expected duration of the events on your shortlist, then re-run the overlap check on the Day plan page.

Who to ask: The individual poker rooms.

What it affects: 28 pairs provably overlap and 34 more are uncertain. Both counts are on the Day plan page.

Q3What is the real nightly cost at The Venetian for my dates?

The official booking calendar loads the stay but every nightly price renders as the placeholder '$XXX'. The property with a zero-minute walk to the tournament room is therefore the one property with no comparable cost in the hotel table.

Ask: Open the booking engine in a browser for your dates, or call the reservations line, and record the nightly price including the resort fee.

Who to ask: The Venetian. The hotel terms and conditions page carries the contact details.

What it affects: The hotel ranking and the Trip cost calculator both exclude it until a rate exists.

Q4Which resort fee actually applies on the booking?

The Caesars booking pages print the resort fee two ways on the same page: the price breakdown uses $50 at Horseshoe and $55 at Caesars Palace, while the fee policy line prints $56.63 and $62.30 including tax. The true nightly cost here uses the price breakdown figure, because that is what the booking engine itself adds.

Ask: Confirm the fee on the checkout summary before paying. It is the last screen before the card is charged.

Who to ask: The booking engine's checkout summary.

What it affects: About $7.53 a night at Horseshoe if the policy line is the charge instead.

Q5What does the Wynn charge for the sample dates?

The official booking URL was opened with the December 10 to 14 window and returned a page describing a one-night stay in mid-November. The price on that page cannot be attached to the requested dates, so no Wynn rate is published.

Ask: Run the same search in a browser for your dates, or call the Wynn reservations line.

Who to ask: The Wynn reservations line.

What it affects: The Wynn's daily tournament slate is also suspended during the WPT World Championship from November 27 to December 21, which covers most of the sample stay.

Q6Is there any tournament that starts early enough for an all-day plan?

The earliest published start anywhere in this dataset is 9:10 a.m., for a turbo milestone satellite on December 12. The earliest single-entry-candidate start is 10:10 a.m. No venue publishes an 8 a.m. start.

Ask: If an early start matters, ask the rooms whether a morning tournament can be added before the published slate.

Who to ask: The individual poker rooms.

What it affects: The Day plan page starts each day at the earliest published start instead of at 8 a.m.

Q7Do South Point and ARIA run daily tournaments that could be planned around?

South Point publishes its schedule and rule sheets as images, so the entry terms could not be read as text. ARIA publishes no tournament times or buy-ins at all and asks guests to call the room to confirm whether daily tournaments are running.

Ask: Read the South Point rule-sheet images, or call both rooms for today's list and the entry rules.

Who to ask: South Point and ARIA poker rooms.

What it affects: Both rooms are excluded from any ranking until their terms are readable.

Q8Which Strip rooms are not covered at all?

This dataset covers The Venetian, Wynn, Bellagio, MGM Grand, Caesars Palace, Horseshoe, the Golden Nugget, South Point and ARIA. Resorts World, Circa, Westgate, the Sahara, the Orleans, the Tropicana, Red Rock, the Palms and the downtown rooms other than the Golden Nugget are not.

Ask: Decide which of those matter to you, then repeat the same structure-sheet check on each one.

Who to ask: Each room's own site.

What it affects: A 'best tournament near the Strip' list built only from this dataset may miss a cheaper single-entry option.
