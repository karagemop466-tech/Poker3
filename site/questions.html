Unanswered questions and current answers

Source links identify the venue page to check; they do not imply that page already answers the missing item.

1 · Is entry strictly limited to one entry per person/event?

Known: current Venetian and Wynn upcoming schedule tables list events, but our reviewed pages do not state a one-entry cap for the candidate rows. Unknown: precise entry cap, re-entry policy, whether another Day 1 flight is permitted.

Ask poker operations to confirm in writing for each event number and each flight. Venetian schedule ↗ · Wynn schedule ↗

2 · Are rebuys, re-entries, add-ons, top-ups, or optional chips offered?

Known: Wynn's daily poster explicitly describes its Friday $240 event as a rebuy. Unknown: exact additional-spend rules for the candidate Venetian and WPT rows.

Ask whether a player can spend more after registering and whether optional purchases change stack size. Wynn official poster ↗ · Venetian structures ↗

3 · What is the final prize pool and payout structure?

Known: some official schedules state guaranteed totals (e.g. $10,000, $15,000, $75,000, $400,000). Unknown: actual entrants, final pool, paid places, rake/staff portion for every candidate. A guarantee is not a forecast.

Request the final event structure and verify the guarantee conditions, fee allocation, and results after the event. Venetian official schedule/structures ↗

4 · Which exact dates are the vacation, how many nights, how many guests?

Known: hotel price depends on stay dates and availability. Unknown: the planned stay dates, length, occupancy, room class, and booking cancellation terms.

A specific itinerary is required to get like-for-like hotel quotes and calculate total / night. Venetian official booking ↗ · Wynn official rooms ↗

5 · What exact taxes, resort charges, and qualifying rates apply?

Known: Venetian, Bellagio, and Wynn general terms publish $55 per night plus tax resort fees. An expired Wynn offer page shows $50. Venetian's poker-rate terms require six hours of live cash play or a buy-in to a $300+ tournament per night. Unknown: date-specific room rate, final tax/fee total, applicable fee exceptions, and final check-out total.

Get a checkout quote for the exact room and stay, and confirm the nightly poker-rate condition. Venetian poker rate terms ↗ · Venetian fee ↗ · Bellagio fee/tax ↗ · Wynn current/general fee terms ↗ · Expired Wynn $50 offer ↗

6 · Which hotel is best on cost and quality, with a walk under 30 minutes?

Known: The Venetian/Palazzo and Wynn/Encore have official poker activity and lodging within their respective resort campuses. Unknown: door-to-room walking route/time, exact room total, and an objective quality rating comparable across hotels.

Measure the route using the exact poker-room entrance and compare review/quality criteria from a chosen independent method; neither has been inferred here. Venetian poker location ↗ · Wynn poker location ↗

7 · Can the plan start at 8 AM and play continuously all day?

Known: the reviewed official Venetian schedule's first selected start is 10:10 AM; Wynn's daily poster has noon and 6 PM starts. Unknown: an earlier event at another Strip room, actual end times, field sizes, and whether any late event can be entered after a morning run.

Request full date-specific calendars plus expected duration/late-registration information from each room. Venetian schedule ↗ · Wynn daily schedule ↗

8 · Are schedule dates, start times, and events still current?

Known: venue pages publish a current series calendar, and reserve rights to modify/cancel events. Unknown: any last-minute changes, registration capacity, and seat availability on a future arrival date.

Recheck the official calendar and call the room shortly before the trip; do not rely on third-party calendar listings for final terms. ARIA's current official poker page says to call or check @ARIAPoker for whether daily tournaments are running. Venetian official page ↗ · Wynn official page ↗ · ARIA current poker page ↗

9 · What is the minimum realistic poker budget?

Known: buy-ins published in the selected rows include $200, $300, $400, and $600; hotel all-in rates remain unknown. Unknown: preferred spend limit, food/transit, taxes, and how many events can actually be played.

Set a bankroll cap before ranking the options; otherwise “best cost” has no actionable ceiling. Official tournament table ↗ · Hotel cost inputs ↗
