Q&A — open questions, information needed and the answers that would close them

This worksheet is the short version of the site's Open questions page: it is written so a decision can be made without reading the whole dashboard. Every answer cell is one of three things and nothing else:

  • ANSWERED — an official venue or property page says it, and the exact quote is on the site.
  • PARTLY — an official source answers part of it, and the rest has to come from the venue.
  • OPEN — no official source found. Nothing is filled in, and the row stays blank on every table.

Third-party pages are never used as an answer. A value cannot be filled in for you.

The five answers that unblock everything else

  1. A dated all-in checkout total for 23–30 Nov 2026 at the poker rate (Q3, Q5) — from the reservation line, with the EPOKR25 code and the play condition stated in writing.
  2. A written one-entry / no-rebuy / no-add-on rule for the 2026 DeepStack IV series (Q10) — from the poker room.
  3. Official finish times, or the room's own expected duration, for sheets 5, 6, 7, 11, 22 and 30 (Q13, Q14).
  4. The refundable-rate deadline, if a refundable rate exists at all (Q9).
  5. Confirmation that the advertised starting rate is available on the trip dates, or the prevailing rate that would replace it if a night fails the play condition (Q4).
