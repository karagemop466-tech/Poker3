The short version

  1. The Venetian is the anchor. Its DeepStack Extravaganza IV series publishes 94 dated tournament rows with a structure sheet for every one of them, which is more official detail than any other room on the Strip publishes.
  2. No sheet in this dataset proves a single total entry. Every structure sheet was read in full. 0 rows are confirmed one-entry; 48 more come from sheets that simply do not mention re-entry, a rebuy, an add-on or a paid chip boost. Silence is reported as silence, not as confirmation.
  3. 9 rows are confirmed extra-spend formats and 36 more are multi-flight, where a player eliminated in one flight may buy into another. Those are kept in their own sections rather than mixed in.
  4. Hotels are ranked on true nightly cost — published rate plus the resort fee plus 13.38% room tax — not on the headline rate. Only 2 of 6 hotels have a date-specific rate that could be read from an official page.
  5. The earliest published start is 9:10 AM (Saturday, December 12, 2026). No venue publishes an 8 a.m. start, so no all-day 8 a.m. schedule is promised here.

How this site decides what is verified

One entry

An event counts as one entry only when the official structure sheet for that event states one entry and contains no re-entry, rebuy, add-on or paid chip boost provision. A sheet that is silent is reported as silent, not as confirmed.

Guarantee ratio

Guaranteed pool divided by entry fee describes what the venue promises to publish. It is never a forecast of a payout. A bounty guarantee includes bounty money, so the cash-pool-to-buy-in ratio is shown next to it.

True cost

True nightly cost = the published nightly rate including the resort fee, plus 13.38% Clark County room tax. It is an arithmetic restatement of published figures, not a quote, and the booking pages say the published rate excludes taxes.

Walking times

Walking times are third-party estimates unless a row says otherwise. No venue publishes walking times to another venue.

Ranking

Tournaments are ranked only after the fee split and the guarantee have both been confirmed on an official structure sheet. Rows with a blank fee or a blank guarantee are not ranked.

Sample dates

No travel dates were supplied by the requester. This is a research sample window chosen because it sits inside the published Venetian DeepStack Extravaganza IV series (Nov 16 - Dec 23, 2026). Every calculator on this site can be pointed at other dates.
