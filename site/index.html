Executive summary · snapshot

One Strip trip, built only from what the venues publish.

Every row on this site was read from an official venue or property page: the Venetian DeepStack Extravaganza structure sheets, the Wynn daily and WPT schedules, the Bellagio weekly structures, the MGM Grand $120 sheet, the Caesars tournament page and each property's own hotel terms. Where a venue prints one buy-in, one start flight and no re-entry, rebuy or add-on clause, the row is marked single entry and links the sheet that says so. Where a room prints a rebuy, a re-entry, an add-on or an optional paid chip boost, the row is isolated in the extra-spend section instead of being quietly mixed in.

Trip fixed for this session: 2026-11-25 → 2026-12-02, seven nights on the Venetian / Palazzo resort. Reasoning below.
What is confirmed and what is not. Tournament fees, guarantees, prize-pool shares, chip counts, level lengths and late-registration cut-offs are confirmed from official sheets for the 37 DeepStack Extravaganza IV events and the daily and weekly room events. Three things are not confirmed and are shown as blanks with a flag rather than filled in: date-specific hotel checkout totals, published tournament finish times, and the entry rules behind the 15 DeepStack NYE sheets and the WPT series rows. Walking minutes are Google Maps walking estimates, because no venue publishes a walking time.

Extra-spend formats, isolated

Wynn Friday rebuy, Bellagio re-entry, MGM Grand and Caesars add-ons

Wynn’s Friday $240 allows unlimited $200 rebuys and one $100 add-on. Bellagio’s Friday and Sunday events allow re-entry until the end of level 8 and offer a voluntary staff gratuity that adds chips. MGM Grand’s $120 sheet counts five entries toward a free Saturday entry. Caesars sells a $50 add-on at the first break. All of these can spend more than the printed buy-in, so none of them is in the single-entry path.

True cost per night, from published parts

The Venetian poker-suite rate

The official rate page publishes starting rates of $119 and $209 for the Venetian and the Palazzo, requires six hours of live cash play or a $300 or higher tournament entry for each night stayed, and the terms add a $55 mandatory resort fee plus 13.38% Clark County room tax. That is a floor of $197.28 a night, computed from published components. It is not a quote, and no property publishes a date-specific total.

Estimate, clearly labelled

No finish times are published anywhere

Sheets publish a start time, a level length and a late-registration cut-off, but never a finish time. The planner therefore estimates a session from the printed cut-off and flags impossible sequences against a stated six-hour assumption and a 30-minute transfer. Every conflict on the day-plan page is an estimate, not an official statement.
