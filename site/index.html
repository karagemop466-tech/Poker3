1

Request exact entry rules from the roomFor each event you might play, confirm in writing: one entry total, no re-entry/rebuy/reload, and no optional add-on. Tournament title and “1Day” do not prove these conditions.

2

Price a defined stayUse the exact dates, number of nights, guest count, room type, and poker-rate eligibility. Compare room rate + resort fee + tax + any extra-guest charge, then compare walk time.

3

Build a schedule from finish estimatesAsk rooms for expected play duration and late-registration rules. Do not treat nearby 5 PM/6 PM starts as playable after a morning event unless the morning event is over.
