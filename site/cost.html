Trip cost

A working estimate for what a stay plus a tournament schedule costs. Every input comes from the official data on this site: the buy-in from the official structure sheet, the nightly rate from the official booking page, and the tax rate from the official hotel terms. Change the dates, the hotel and the number of entries and the totals update. The result is an arithmetic estimate, not a quote.

How the estimate is built

  • Room cost = true nightly cost × nights, where true nightly cost is the dated published rate including the resort fee, plus 13.38% room tax.
  • Tournament cost = entries × the buy-in on the official structure sheet. Multi-flight and seat-prize events are counted in the extra-spend field rather than assumed away.
  • Parking is not included. The Venetian publishes $20 a day for registered guests and $40 a day for valet, and other properties publish their own rates. Add them from the parking column on the Hotels page.
  • Not included: travel, food, the $50 plus tax resort fee is already inside the nightly rate, and the final folio can differ from this estimate because the booking pages say the published rate excludes taxes.
