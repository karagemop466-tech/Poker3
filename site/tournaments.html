Reading the columns

Guaranteed pool ÷ fee

A descriptive ratio, never a forecast

The figure divides the published guaranteed pool by the published entry fee. It is a way to compare the published numbers with each other. It says nothing about expected value, because a guarantee is a floor paid only if the field fills, and bounty guarantees include money paid as bounties rather than as prize pool.

Estimated finish

Derived from published data, labelled as an estimate

When a sheet prints its late-registration cut-off time, the session cannot end before that moment. The estimate adds two more levels. With no printed cut-off time, the planner falls back to a stated six-hour session. The transfer allowance between rooms is 30 minutes.

Entry check

What the sheet actually prints

“No re-entry, rebuy or add-on printed” is a statement about the document, not about the room’s policy. Where a sheet prints re-entry, rebuy, add-on, a paid chip boost or a separate start flight, the row is labelled accordingly and kept out of the single-entry filter.

Blank cells

Left blank on purpose

Horseshoe publishes its six daily start times but no buy-in, level or guarantee. The ARIA guide is undated. The Bellagio Saturday sheet prints three different buy-ins. In each case the field stays empty and the row carries a link to the page for manual review.
