What the entry-rule column means

One entry, confirmed by the structure sheet
The sheet states one entry and contains no re-entry, rebuy, add-on or paid chip boost provision.
Single entry not stated on the sheet
The sheet was read in full and does not mention re-entry, a rebuy, an add-on or a paid chip boost. That is not proof of a single entry, so the row is not called confirmed. Ask the room if it matters.
Multi-flight: one entry per flight
The sheet names separate starting flights and only bars players who bag chips for Day 2 from entering a later flight. A player eliminated in one flight may buy into another.
Extra-spend format
Either the sheet sells further buy-ins outright, or the prize is a seat, so every extra buy-in is another chance at the same prize.
Team format
One buy-in covers more than one player, so the per-player cost is not the buy-in.
