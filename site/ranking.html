Ranking · — rows ranked

Ranked only after the fee and the guarantee are both confirmed.

A row appears here only when an official source prints both its entry fee and its guaranteed prize pool, and only when the fee line is confirmed on a structure sheet rather than inferred from the event name. Everything else stays on the tournament table with the blank cell and the flag that explains it.

Two lists, never one. Bounty and multi-flight events are ranked in their own tables below, because their guarantee includes money paid out as bounties or spans several starting flights, so it is not the same quantity as a single-day freezeout's prize pool. Comparing the two side by side would compare different things.

Single-buy-in, single-flight events

One printed buy-in, one printed start flight, no re-entry, rebuy, add-on or second-flight clause on the sheet. Sorted by the published pool ÷ fee ratio, highest first. The sheet-screening test does not establish a maximum number of entries per player — that remains open on F31.

Bounty events, ranked separately

The guarantee printed on a bounty event includes the money paid out as bounties, so its ratio is not comparable with a cash prize pool. Each row names the bounty structure exactly as the source prints it.

Multi-flight and series events, ranked separately

These events publish several starting flights, so one player can buy more than one entry and the guarantee is shared across every flight. They are listed for comparison only and are never offered as a single-entry option.

How to read the ranking

Step 1

Total entry fee confirmed

A row is ranked only when the fee is printed by the source, either as the buy-in or as an itemised split ("$90 of the $600 Buy-In is withheld for house fees"). A buy-in that appears only in an event name is not treated as confirmed.

Step 2

Guarantee confirmed

A guarantee printed for a different event number, or a guarantee that appears only in a summary sentence, is not used. Where a sheet prints several guarantees for several event numbers, the row takes the one that names its own event.

Step 3

Ratio, labelled as a ratio

Guaranteed pool ÷ entry fee. A 250× figure on a $300 event with a $75,000 guarantee means exactly that: $75,000 divided by $300. It is not a return, an expectation or a probability, and it does not account for the share of the field that is paid.

Step 4

Still conditional

No reviewed structure sheet prints a maximum number of entries per player. A ranked row is therefore a research shortlist item, not an eligible single-entry event, until the room confirms that rule in writing.
