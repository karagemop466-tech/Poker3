The official 2026 page says poker suite rates start as low as $119 and $209 across the two properties. Eligibility: six hours of active live cash play or a buy-in to a $300-or-higher tournament for each night. If the requirement is missed, that night's rate adjusts to prevailing rate. A $200 tournament alone does not meet the stated tournament threshold.
