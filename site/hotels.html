Hotels · published components only

What a night actually costs, and what it is worth.

No reviewed property publishes a date-specific checkout total. What they do publish is the pieces: a room rate or a published starting rate, a mandatory daily resort fee, a tax rule, a parking price, a cancellation deadline and the terms for holding a booking. This page puts those pieces in the same columns so the properties can be compared like for like, and it keeps every observation in a dated log instead of overwriting one number with a newer one. Walking times come from Google Maps walking estimates captured on , because no property publishes a walking time to its own poker room.

Recommended

The Venetian or The Palazzo poker suite

Both properties are on the same resort as the main poker room. The official poker-room rate page publishes starting rates of $119 and $209 and requires six hours of live cash play or a $300 or higher tournament entry for each night stayed, completed before 2 a.m. on the last night. The mandatory $55 resort fee and 13.38% Clark County room tax take the floor to $197.28 a night. The Palazzo is a four-minute walk from the Venetian poker room.

Both figures are published starting rates, not a quote for your dates, and the page does not map each starting rate to a named tower. That is why the room-rate column shows a range.

Quality floor

What “good enough” means here

No reviewed property publishes a comparable quality score, so the quality column is blank rather than invented. The floor applied instead is evidence-based: the property must publish its own poker-room rate, keep the poker room on site, and publish its resort fee, tax and cancellation terms. On that test the Venetian and the Palazzo are the only properties that qualify, and both are the same resort.

Wynn publishes the clearest fee and cancellation terms of any property reviewed, and its rooms page links Forbes Travel Guide, but it publishes no room price, so no total can be computed.

What still has to be confirmed

  • Your dates, number of nights and number of guests. Every rate is date- and occupancy-dependent.
  • The tax percentage for Wynn and Bellagio: both publish “plus applicable tax” without the number.
  • Resort fee, tax and parking for MGM Grand, Caesars Palace, Horseshoe and ARIA, which were not captured in this pass.
  • Whether the poker-suite rate is available on your dates; the Venetian states that rates are provided up to 150 days out.
