Monitoring — scheduled re-check of official sources

A GitHub Actions job (.github/workflows/daily-check.yml) runs every morning (12:05 UTC ≈ 4:05 AM Las Vegas), re-fetches every official URL listed below, stores dated text snapshots, and compares them with the previous snapshot. Rows whose source text changed are marked on this page and in site/data/changes.json; the job commits the updated snapshots and badges to main, which redeploys this site.

What the morning feed covers

  • Daily schedule feed: Venetian DSE schedule (web + PDF), Wynn poker page, Wynn daily poster PDF, WPT schedule PDF, Bellagio poker page, ARIA poker page, Horseshoe page — each snapshotted under snapshots/<date>/.
  • Change detection: SHA-256 of normalized text per URL; any change flips the badge below to “source changed — re-check”.
  • Row marking: every tournament/hotel row records lastChecked; when its source changes, the row is listed in changes.json and shown here as needing re-verification.
