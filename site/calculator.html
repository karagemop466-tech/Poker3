Trip cost, computed from published parts.

The formula is the one in the brief: estimated cost = entries × buy-ins + nights × true hotel cost. The defaults are the recommended trip — eight verified single-entry entries and seven nights on the Venetian / Palazzo poker rate — priced from the official starting rate, the published $55 resort fee and the published 13.38% Clark County room tax. Change any field and the arithmetic is redone in the browser.

This is a floor, not a quote. No property publishes a date-specific total for the recommended window: the official booking engine returns $XXX until its prices load. The floor below is built from the published starting rate and is labelled as a floor everywhere it appears.
The $119 room rate is conditional, and failing the condition reprices the whole stay. The official poker-rate page makes it depend on six hours of active live cash gameplay, or a buy-in to a $300 or higher tournament, for each night stayed, completed before 2 a.m. on your last night. It states that for any night you do not complete those requirements your nightly rate “will be adjusted to the prevailing rate”. Two further official limits apply: the poker rate “cannot be combined with any other promotional rates”, and rates are only published “out to a maximum of 150 days from the current date”. So treat the hotel line as the cost if you qualify every night — a night off is not a free night, it is a repriced night. Official poker-rate page → (Flagged as F54 on the Flagged for review page.)
On this particular plan, only five of the seven nights can be qualified by a tournament. The stay runs 25–30 November and 1 December. Five of those nights have a $300+ single-entry event ($400, $300, $600, $600, $300). Monday 30 November and Tuesday 1 December have no $300+ single-entry event at all — the largest published single-entry event on both dates is $200 — so those two nights need six hours of live cash play or they are repriced. The seven-night figure below is therefore the cost if every night qualifies, and it is optimistic for this plan by however much two nights of not qualifying cost. Tournament entries

Hotel stay

$119 and $209 are the two official starting rates on the poker rate page: "These rates will start as low as $119 and $209 and can be higher." The page does not say which figure belongs to which tower. $55 is the resort fee, "plus tax"; 13.38% is the Clark County room tax, applied to the rate and the fee. $20 is the registered-guest self-parking fee. Qualifying play is six hours of live cash, or a $300+ tournament entry, per night stayed.

How to use it before booking

  1. Get a date-specific checkout total for the same stay and occupancy from the hotel's official booking page. Rates are date- and availability-dependent.
  2. Split that total into room rate, mandatory resort fee and tax, so hotels can be compared like for like.
  3. Replace the eight-event default with the entries you would actually play, and remember that re-entry, rebuy and add-on formats may cost more than one buy-in.
  4. Re-confirm the official schedule, the rate and the cancellation deadline before paying anything non-refundable.
