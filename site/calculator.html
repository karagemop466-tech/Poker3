How to use it

  1. Get a date-specific checkout total for the same stay and occupancy from the hotel’s official booking page (rates are date- and availability-dependent).
  2. Split it into room rate, mandatory resort fee, and tax. Compare like-for-like across hotels.
  3. Add entries × buy-ins for every event you plan to enter. Re-entry/rebuy events may cost more than one buy-in per day.
  4. Only book after confirming entry rules, finish times, walking routes, and cancellation deadlines from official sources.
