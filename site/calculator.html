Trip cost, computed from published parts.

The core formula is entries × average buy-in + nights × true hotel cost. Parking is shown separately and added only to the optional all-in figure. The defaults reflect the seven planned primary-event candidates across the selected trip dates and seven nights on the Venetian / Palazzo poker rate, priced from the official starting rate, the published $55 resort fee and the published 13.38% Clark County room tax. These are provisional candidates, not confirmed single-entry events: no reviewed sheet states a maximum of one entry per player (F31). Change any field and the arithmetic is redone in the browser.

The rows below are a floor, not a quote. The official booking engine quoted a dated checkout total for 23-30 November 2026 on 2026-11-17 ($4,010.55, shown separately below). The floor is built from the published starting rate and is not a date-specific rate.

Hotel stay

Or load a property's own published components

Each button copies that property's published room rate, mandatory resort fee, tax rate and parking fee into the boxes above. No value is filled in that the property does not publish. A property whose own quote already includes the resort fee is flagged instead of loaded, because this calculator adds the fee itself from a fee-exclusive room rate.

$119 and $209 are the two official starting rates on the poker rate page: "These rates will start as low as $119 and $209 and can be higher." The page does not say which figure belongs to which tower. $55 is the resort fee, "plus tax"; 13.38% is the Clark County room tax, applied to the rate and the fee. $20 is the registered-guest self-parking fee. Qualifying play is six hours of live cash, or a $300+ tournament entry, per night stayed.

Do not paste a booking-calendar figure into the room-rate box. This calculator adds the resort fee itself, and several properties already fold the fee into the price they display: The Venetian's calendar says "Pricing shown is best available suite and rate, includes daily resort fee, excludes taxes", and Caesars Palace says "Rates shown reflect the lowest available rate and include applicable resort fees" (both pages re-read 2026-11-17). Entering one of those figures here double-counts the fee — see F69, which shows the itemised nightly lines and the calendar cells disagreeing on the same seven nights. Parking has a published waiver, and it is not applied here. The resort's poker-room self-parking page (read 2026-11-17) prints: "Players in the poker room who play three hours or more in tournament or cash games will receive complimentary self-parking for the day. Players must visit a supervisor at the end of their session to confirm qualifications for complimentary self-parking." The same page prints the $20 registered-guest fee above, warns that "Event parking rates may be in effect", and says rates are "subject to change". No official source publishes a tournament finish time (F16), so a three-hour session cannot be confirmed from a schedule: the $20 stays in the total, and you may set this field to 0 for a day the room has confirmed for you. See flagged item F86.

The poker rate is conditional, as the official poker-suite rate page prints it (re-read 2026-11-17): it is booked by phone under code EPOKR25 with a Venetian Rewards account tracking play; "For any nights you fail to complete these requirements, your nightly suite rate will be adjusted to the prevailing rate, and you will not receive the poker suite rate discount"; "The poker rate cannot be combined with any other promotional rates"; and "Future rates are only provided out to a maximum of 150 days from the current date," with blackout dates and occupancy-based availability. The nightly figure this calculator produces from $119 is therefore a floor for a qualifying player, not a quote (flag F54). The fee basis behind it is contested by two official pages: the Resort Fee page says “Rates include a daily Resort Fee of $55 plus tax per night” — the fee is chargeable on top of the rate, which is what this calculator does — while the booking calendar says its own prices include the fee. The conservative reading is kept; if the poker rate is fee-inclusive the true floor is about $43 a night lower (flag F51).

How to use it before booking

  1. Get a date-specific checkout total for the same stay and occupancy from the hotel's official booking page. Rates are date- and availability-dependent.
  2. Split that total into room rate, mandatory resort fee and tax, so hotels can be compared like for like.
  3. Replace the seven-candidate default with the entries you would actually play, and remember that re-entry, rebuy and add-on formats may cost more than one buy-in.
  4. Re-confirm the official schedule, the rate and the cancellation deadline before paying anything non-refundable.
