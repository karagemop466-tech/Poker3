How to use this plan

  1. Play the primary event. It has the best confirmed ratio that day among single-start, sheet-confirmed events.
  2. If you bust before the fallback's late-registration closes (shown on its row), you can switch; if not, your day ends with the primary.
  3. Overlap badges mean the earlier event plausibly runs past the later event's registration window — treat those as either/or, never both.

Every table row links its official venue source. Values that cannot be confirmed from an official source are blank and flagged on Flagged for review. Guarantees are not forecasts. Ratio = guaranteed pool ÷ buy-in, descriptive only.
