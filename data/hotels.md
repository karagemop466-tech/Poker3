**Research date:** 2026-11-16
**Tournament venues:** Venetian (3355 S Las Vegas Blvd), Wynn (3131 S Las Vegas Blvd), ARIA (3730 S Las Vegas Blvd)
**Walking-distance limit:** ≤ 30 minutes on foot (~1.5 miles)
**Priority:** cost and quality of hotel are higher priority than distance; distance must be reasonable (≤ 30 min walk)

## True cost formula
True cost per night = room rate + nightly resort fee + Clark County lodging tax on the room rate (+ parking, if you drive).
- Clark County lodging tax commonly cited as 13.38% (third-party sources; verify with hotel).
- Resort fees are MANDATORY daily fees charged on top of the advertised room rate, whether or not you use the amenities.
- Room rates are dynamic and were NOT confirmed from official hotel booking systems for specific November/December 2026 dates — room-rate cells are left blank and flagged unverified per the verification policy.

## Walking-distance notes (estimates — verify with a map before booking)
- All three tournament venues (Venetian, Wynn, ARIA) are on the Las Vegas Strip. Staying AT the venue hotel = 0-minute walk.
- Walk-time estimates are derived from resort addresses on the Strip (≈20–25 min per mile on the Strip, accounting for signals/crowds) and are NOT from official sources. Flagged as estimates.
- Venetian to Wynn ≈ 0.6 mi (~12–15 min); Venetian to ARIA ≈ 1.3 mi (~25–28 min — at the edge of the 30-minute limit); Wynn to ARIA ≈ 0.7 mi (~15 min).
- Pedestrian bridges cross the Strip at several points (e.g., TI↔Palazzo/Venetian), but Clark County bridges are described as outdoor, not air-conditioned — factor in weather (November–December Las Vegas evenings are cool).

## Hotel recommendation logic
- Cost & quality first: For a Strip base with on-site tournament play, The Venetian and Wynn are the tournament venues themselves (0-minute walk) but carry the highest fees ($55+tax + $20 parking at Venetian). Wynn offers free self-parking for guests.
- Lowest true-cost options found (fees only; room rates unverified): Best Western Plus Casino Royale (no resort fee, free parking), Polo Towers ($28 fee — lowest fee), TI ($49.95 fee, free parking).
- All listed hotels are within ~30 minutes' walk of at least one tournament venue except Polo Towers (off-strip; flagged).
