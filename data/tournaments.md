## How entry type was classified
- **Single entry (freezeout):** The official Venetian structure sheet for the event contains no re-entry, rebuy, or add-on provision, and the event is designated "1Day" or is a 2Day event with no multi-flight re-entry clause. Multi-flight Venetian sheets all contain rule 08 ("Any player who bags chips for Day 2 may not play a subsequent flight"), which means re-entry across flights IS permitted until a player bags — those events are listed in the separate re-entry section below.
- **Re-entry / multi-buy-in:** Events where the official structure explicitly permits re-entry across flights (Venetian rule 08), explicit subsequent buy-ins (Venetian structure 08: "All subsequent buy in's after the initial buy in will be $200"), or unlimited re-entry (WPT Super Gold Million). These can give an advantage to players who spend more money and are kept in a separate section.
- **Satellite / qualifier:** Milestone satellites that award a direct entry seat into a larger event. Treated as qualifiers, not main events.
