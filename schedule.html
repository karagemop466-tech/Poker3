Important constraint — no 8 AM tournament start exists

The earliest official tournament start in the Venetian DSE IV schedule is 10:10 AM (RaceDay Stack events Nov 19–21, Dec 2 Seniors, etc.). There is no 8 AM poker tournament start in the published official schedule. An "8 AM start" day means: arrive at the poker room by 8:00 AM, complete registration, then play cash games or wait until the first tournament at 10:10 AM. Cash game availability is not verified from an official source — see Flagged for Review.

Schedule rules (from official sources)

  • 21+ years of age; Venetian Rewards card required to play ([Venetian structure sheets](https://www.venetianlasvegas.com/content/dam/vlvweb/casino/poker/deepstack/2026/structures/dse_nov_2026-structure_1.pdf)).
  • TDA rules apply to all Venetian Poker Room tournaments.
  • Management reserves the right to cancel or change tournaments — confirm times before traveling.
  • Single entry: one entry per event; busting out ends that event. Entering multiple flights of the same event would violate the single-entry rule — see the separate section on the Tournaments page.
  • Earliest official start: 10:10 AM. No 8 AM tournament start exists in the published schedule.
