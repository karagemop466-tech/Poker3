What this site is

A planning resource for a Las Vegas Strip poker vacation built around single-entry tournaments only — no double buy-ins, no re-entry, no add-ons, and no events where spending more money buys an advantage. Tournaments that allow multiple entries are kept in a separate section on the Tournaments page so they can be reviewed and excluded deliberately.

Headline findings

  • Main series: Venetian DeepStack Extravaganza IV, Nov 16 – Dec 23, 2026 at The Venetian Poker Room (3355 S Las Vegas Blvd), more than $3.6M in guaranteed prize pools across 64 events. [official schedule]
  • 53 single-entry (1Day) events captured from Nov 18 – Dec 23, every one linked to its official structure sheet. Every table row carries a source link; nothing is inferred.
  • Best value (guarantee ÷ buy-in): Event #18/#58 $200 NLH PokerAtlas Valuetown Bounty $20K GTD (Nov 28 / Dec 19) at 100× buy-in; Event #36 $800 NLH Bounty $75K GTD (Dec 9) has the largest guarantee ($75,000) at 93.75×.
  • Second venue: Wynn Las Vegas WPT World Championship Festival, Nov 27 – Dec 21, 2026. Nearly all WPT events allow re-entry or multiple flights, so they are in the separate "not single entry" section. [official WPT press release]
  • Hotels: The Venetian/Palazzo are on-site at the tournament venue; Wynn/Encore are one pedestrian bridge away (~0.5 mile). Both charge a $55/night + tax resort fee (official). Room rates for Nov–Dec 2026 are not yet published — left blank and flagged, per the no-inference rule.
