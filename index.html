⚠️ Nothing here is inferred. Every value either comes from a linked official/public source or is left blank and marked UNVERIFIED. Confirm schedules, entry policies, and rates directly with each poker room and hotel before booking or traveling.

What this site covers
  • Single-entry tournaments only (main table) — buy-in once, no rebuy/add-on. Events where spending more money can buy an advantage are in a separate section.
  • Venues: Venetian DeepStack Extravaganza IV (Nov 16 – Dec 23, 2026), WPT World Championship at Wynn (Nov 27 – Dec 21, 2026), plus standing daily tournaments at Wynn/Encore, ARIA, Horseshoe, and MGM Grand on the Strip.
  • Hotels: Venetian, Wynn/Encore, ARIA, Bellagio, Vdara, The Cosmopolitan, Caesars Palace, Paris — all on or beside the Strip, within ~30 minutes' walking distance of the tournament rooms.
  • True cost per night: base rate + mandatory resort fee + 13.38% Clark County lodging tax. Future-dated room rates for Nov–Dec 2026 are not yet published; where a source could not confirm a value it is left blank and flagged.
  • All-day schedule: built so you can play from the earliest tournament (9:00 AM at Horseshoe — no Strip room researched starts at 8:00 AM) through late-night events.
How to read the verification flags
  • VERIFIED — value confirmed from an official or public listing source; source link is on the row.
  • UNVERIFIED — could not be confirmed from an official source; value left blank or shown as a dated sample only.
  • CONFLICT — two or more sources disagree; all conflicting values are listed with their sources so you can decide.

See the Flagged for Review page for the complete list of unverified items and irregularities, each with source links for your manual review.

Important — single entry means single entry. Most Las Vegas daily tournaments advertise "re-entry" (you may buy in again if you bust out), which is spending more money for an advantage. Those rooms are listed in the separate rebuy/add-on section, not the single-entry table. Entry policy could not be verified from published sources for most events — treat every row as "confirm with the poker room" until you hear otherwise.
