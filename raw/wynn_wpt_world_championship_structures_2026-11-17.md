## What the document does **not** contain

- No section for the **$10,400 WPT World Championship** (Dec 13–20) or any of its flights, Day 2–5 or final
  table. Its rows therefore keep the unverified label.
- No section for the **$1,100 NLH Turbo** on Dec 18, the **$3,000 / $10,500 / $25,800** high-roller events, the
  **Super Gold Million Mystery Bounty** (Dec 10–14), the **Seniors** events, the **Ladies** championship, the
  **NLH Mystery Bounty** (Dec 19–21), the **Wynn Mixed Championship**, the **Limit Omaha 8 Championship**, the
  **TORSE Championship**, or any **Milestone Satellite**.
- No add-on provision anywhere. Each section lists registration, stack, level length, breaks, table size and
  payouts; none prints a rebuy, an add-on or a paid chip boost.
- No finish time for any event.
- No maximum number of entries per player across the series as a whole. Even the Prime Championship's explicit
  "One (1) Entry and One (1) re-entry for each starting flight" is a per-flight limit, not a series limit.

## Discrepancies this read confirms (already published as flags)

- **F60** — section 8 prints a $1,100 buy-in whose components add to $1,105.
- **F34** — the schedule PDF prints a "No Limit Hold'em Day 2" for the $800 event on Mon Nov 30 1 p.m., while
  section 5 prints that event's Day 2 on Thu Dec 3 1 p.m. The conflicting row is left unmatched on purpose.
- **F61** — the schedule PDF's "Dec. 18 Wednesday" block is probably Dec 16; nothing in this structures
  document is dated Dec 16, so the read neither confirms nor contradicts it.
