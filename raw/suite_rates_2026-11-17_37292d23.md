## Poker Suite Rate Information Guide

We offer suite rates for poker players who are interested in staying in either The Venetian or The Palazzo. These rates will start as low as $119 and $209 and can be higher. Future rates are only provided out to a maximum of 150 days from the current date. Rates are subject to qualifying poker play and availability based on hotel occupancy. Blackout dates may apply. Rates are based on single/double occupancy in a Luxury King Suite in The Venetian or The Palazzo. There is a $55 resort fee per night stayed. Amenities include access to Canyon Ranch® fitness facility for 2, internet access, unlimited local and toll-free calls, and free boarding pass printing. Upgrades to your suite, including movement to a Two Queen Luxury Suite, view suite, Prestige and Concierge level, greater than double occupancy, or any other upgrades, are permitted and will be charged in addition to the stated base rate.

The poker rate cannot be combined with any other promotional rates. In order to qualify for these rates, the first step is to book your suite through The Venetian Resort Las Vegas by calling 866.255.8936 and mentioning code EPOKR25. Please have your Venetian Rewards account number ready, as this will be used to track your poker play during your stay. If you do not have a Venetian Rewards account, you can sign up for one at our website. If you have a Venetian Rewards account but do not know the account number, we can assist you over the phone. Please also provide the information, including Venetian Rewards accounts, for any additional guests who will be staying in your suite. Failure to do so will result in their play not being included toward the poker rate.

Each time you play, you must provide your Venetian Rewards card to the dealer. This card will be able to track how many hours you have played poker. In addition, the player's card will earn you $2 in rewards per hour played. These rewards are redeemable at many different outlets throughout The Venetian Resort, including Grand Lux Cafe, which can be served tableside while you play. Poker Rewards can also be used toward tournament buy-ins, in-room poker merchandise, and massage services in the poker room. A complete list of participating outlets is available inside the poker room. You are responsible for ensuring you provide your Venetian Rewards card each time you play and are swiped in for the duration of each play session. If you have any questions or discrepancies, they must be brought to a Supervisor's attention prior to leaving the room for that session of play.

During your stay, you will be required to play six hours of active live cash gameplay or buy in to a $300 or higher tournament for each night stayed. Poker play for any guests listed in your suite will also count toward these requirements. These requirements must be met prior to 2 a.m. the last night stayed. For any nights you fail to complete these requirements, your nightly suite rate will be adjusted to the prevailing rate, and you will not receive the poker suite rate discount. Lack of qualification will be automatically applied and sent to the front desk, such that when you go to checkout, your rate will be accurately reflected.

However, if at any time you have any questions, including hours played, you are welcome to speak to a poker room Supervisor or Shift Manager.

If you have any other questions, comments, or concerns, please email us at lv_cas_poker@venetianlasvegas.com or call the poker room at 702.414.7657, and we will be happy to help. We look forward to seeing you in our poker room and hope you choose to stay in our luxurious resort while enjoying your stay in Las Vegas.
