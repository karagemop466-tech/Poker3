## Body text, copied verbatim

> Venetian Rewards Sapphire members and above receive complimentary self-parking. Venetian Rewards Ruby members and
> above continue to receive complimentary valet parking. Nevada residents receive three hours of complimentary
> self-parking by scanning their valid Nevada Driver's License at a self-parking kiosk.  The Nevada Resident rate is
> not available when Event Parking Rates are in effect.

> The daily fee for registered hotel guests is $20/day, seven days a week. For non-hotel guests, the fee is $20/day
> Monday – Thursday, and $25/day Friday – Sunday. Valet parking is $40/day.

> Players in the poker room who play three hours or more in tournament or cash games will receive complimentary
> self-parking for the day. Players must visit a supervisor at the end of their session to confirm qualifications for
> complimentary self-parking. In addition, players in the poker room who have played 75 cash game hours or 15
> tournaments in the past 12 months can email Thomas.LaRosa@venetianlasvegas.com to have their play validated and
> their Venetian Rewards tier status upgraded to Sapphire to receive the benefit complimentary self-parking. Parking
> rates subject to change at the discretion of The Venetian Resort Las Vegas.

> Event parking rates may be in effect and are subject to change; check posted signage for additional details. Parking
> rates subject to change at the discretion of The Venetian Resort Las Vegas.

## What this page does and does not establish

Established, in the property's own words:
1. Registered hotel guests pay $20/day for self-parking, seven days a week (the figure the trip-cost model charges).
2. Non-hotel guests pay $20/day Monday–Thursday and $25/day Friday–Sunday; valet is $40/day.
3. **A player who plays three hours or more in the poker room, in tournament or cash games, receives complimentary
   self-parking for the day.** The condition is validated by a supervisor at the end of the session.
4. Venetian Rewards Sapphire and above: complimentary self-parking. Ruby and above: complimentary valet.
5. Nevada residents: three hours complimentary self-parking by scanning a Nevada driver's licence at a self-parking
   kiosk; not available while Event Parking Rates are in effect.
6. A tier route exists for regulars: 75 cash-game hours or 15 tournaments in the past 12 months, validated by email
   to the address printed on the page, upgrades to Sapphire, which carries complimentary self-parking.
7. Both parking and event-parking rates are explicitly "subject to change at the discretion of The Venetian Resort
   Las Vegas", and posted signage governs during events.

Not established by this page, and therefore not published anywhere on the site as a fact:
- Whether a specific tournament session will exceed three hours. No official source publishes finish times (F16), so
  the waiver is **not** applied to any published total; the paid figure stays in the model and the waiver is published
  beside it as a conditional, verbatim, sourced benefit.
- Whether the waiver covers a registered guest's overnight parking charge as billed to the room folio, or only the
  self-parking garage at exit. The page says "for the day" and says nothing about folio billing. Left blank.
- Whether the waiver applies during DeepStack Extravaganza dates (the page warns Event Parking Rates may be in effect;
  no calendar of event-parking dates is published on this page).
