Calculation, once sourced: (room charges for all nights + mandatory resort fees for all nights + mandatory taxes/fees) ÷ number of nights. Keep parking, optional services and refundable deposits separately identified; do not silently mix them into nightly rate.

How to choose

  1. Get date-specific total price from each hotel’s own booking flow, with the same room/guest assumptions.
  2. Record resort fee, taxes and any mandatory destination fee separately and cite the official checkout/fee page.
  3. Measure walk route from hotel entrance to the confirmed poker-room entrance; account for casino interior and pedestrian crossings. Route estimates are not official hotel facts.
  4. Rank price/quality first, then eliminate venues over the 30-minute walking limit.
