How to compute true cost yourself: get the official direct-booking room rate from the hotel's website for your dates, then add: resort fee + (room rate × 13.38% estimated Clark County lodging tax) + parking (if driving). All resort-fee figures above come from third-party travel sites — confirm directly with each hotel before booking.

Walking-distance notes (estimates — verify with a map before booking)

  • All three tournament venues (Venetian, Wynn, ARIA) are on the Las Vegas Strip. Staying at the venue hotel = 0-minute walk.
  • Walk-time estimates are derived from resort addresses on the Strip (≈20–25 min per mile, accounting for signals/crowds) and are NOT from official sources.
  • Venetian to Wynn ≈ 0.6 mi (~12–15 min); Venetian to ARIA ≈ 1.3 mi (~25–28 min — at the edge of the 30-minute limit); Wynn to ARIA ≈ 0.7 mi (~15 min).
  • Pedestrian bridges cross the Strip at several points (e.g., TI↔Palazzo/Venetian), but Clark County bridges are outdoor, not air-conditioned — November–December evenings are cool.

Flagged items (see Flagged for Review page): room rates for specific dates; all resort fees (third-party sources only); Clark County lodging tax rate; walking times; ARIA/Vdara/Bellagio fees and parking. Nothing is inferred or filled in.

Hotel recommendation logic

  • Cost & quality first: staying AT the tournament venue (Venetian, Wynn, ARIA) = 0-minute walk, but Venetian/Wynn carry the highest fees ($55+tax/night; Venetian adds $20/day guest self-parking). Wynn offers free self-parking for guests.
  • Lowest true-cost options found (fees only; rates unverified): Best Western Plus Casino Royale (no resort fee, free parking), Polo Towers ($28 fee — lowest found), Treasure Island ($49.95 fee, free parking).
  • All listed hotels are within ~30 minutes' walk of at least one tournament venue except Polo Towers (off-Strip; flagged).
