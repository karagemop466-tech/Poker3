Executive summary

Scope: the Venetian (DeepStack Extravaganza IV) and Wynn Las Vegas (WPT World Championship), plus official resort-fee terms for three hotels. Retrieved 2026-11-16.

1. Live and upcoming tournaments (official)

  • Venetian DeepStack Extravaganza IV, Nov 16 to Dec 23, 2026: 64 numbered events, 83 flights, 7 of them satellites. 11 restart or final-table rows are listed separately.
  • Wynn WPT World Championship, Nov 27 to Dec 21, 2026: 116 buy-in rows plus 20 restart or final-table rows, from the official schedule PDF.
  • Wynn daily tournaments: five recurring slots on the official poster. No dates are printed, so they are shown separately.
  • WSOP: no Las Vegas event in this window on the official WSOP schedule (flag F19).

2. Single-entry vs. re-entry (kept separate)

  • Venetian flights are the single-entry list. Only 24 of them are on structure sheets that were read, and none of those sheets states a re-entry rule. The other 59 are marked 'not yet checked' (flag F07).
  • Wynn: 4 Nov 27 rows state unlimited re-entry until level 9 in the official structure sheet. They appear under 'Re-entry & unverified', not in single-entry.
  • The other 112 Wynn buy-in rows are not verified (flag F11).
  • No official event in the two schedules is described as a double buy-in or add-on. Events that were not checked may still have one.

3. Best value (calculated from printed numbers)

Guarantee ÷ buy-in shows how large the printed guarantee is against the entry fee. It is not expected return: field sizes are not published.

  • Highest guarantee ÷ buy-in: $800 NO LIMIT HOLD'EM 1A, Thu Dec 3 at 11:10 AM: $500,000 on $800 = 625x.
  • Largest guarantees: $2,200 NO LIMIT HOLD'EM 1A POKERATLAS CHAMPIONSHIP TITLE EVENT ($1,000,000) and the other $1,000,000+ events on the Best picks page.
  • Low buy-in with a guarantee (at or under $200): 15 flights, all bounty or PKO events or the PokerAtlas Valuetown event.

4. The 8 AM all-day goal

  • The earliest printed Venetian start is 9:10 AM (Sat Dec 12†, a satellite). Most flights start at 10:10 or 11:10 AM.
  • No finish times are printed, so the site cannot show that two events fit back to back. The Day schedule page lists every printed start so you can plan.

5. Hotels

  • Resort fee: $55 plus tax per night at The Venetian (official resort-fee page), Wynn (official terms, bookings on or after Jan 15, 2025) and Resorts World (official FAQ).
  • Resorts World's official offer waives the fee on select dates through Dec 28, 2026; blackout dates may apply and room tax still applies.
  • Walking times, room rates, tax and quality ratings are not verified and are left blank. The true nightly cost cannot be computed without a room rate; the calculator on the Hotels page does the sum once you enter one.

6. Biggest gaps

  • Clark County tax (official page 404), room rates, walking times (flags F02, F15, F16).
  • Other Strip rooms and Resorts World tournaments (F13, F14).
  • Re-entry rules for 59 Venetian flights and 112 Wynn rows (F07, F11).

Method

Each row was taken from the official PDF or page and carries its link. Venetian and Wynn rows were parsed from the verbatim official text stored in raw/. Dagger (†) marks a date inferred from row order. Calendar weekdays were checked; a printed weekday that conflicts is flagged. Nothing was filled in from a third-party source.

Figures come only from the official venue and resort pages and PDFs linked on each row (see Sources), retrieved 2026-11-16. Blank = not verified. Nothing is estimated. Ratios (guarantee divided by buy-in) are calculated by the site from official numbers and are not expected return. Check every value on the official page before you enter or book.
