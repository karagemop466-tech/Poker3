Suggestions for improving this site

Suggestions are deliberately separated from implemented features. Choose the most useful path before expanding the planner:

1 · Date-driven refreshAdd a date selector and re-check official room calendars and hotel checkout rates for the chosen dates.
2 · Rules evidenceStore source excerpt, retrieval date, single-entry status, re-entry/add-on status, fee and guarantee separately for every event.
3 · Schedule optimizerOnce start/finish and travel times are sourced, show only non-overlapping itineraries and flag unverified transitions.
4 · Hotel total calculatorCompare consistent room/guest assumptions, mandatory fees, taxes, cancellation terms and route walking time.
5 · Change monitoringStamp each source check and flag stale schedules; never carry forward old dates as current events.

Decision gate

Do not book based on this snapshot. A reasonable recommendation requires at minimum confirmed dates, current official event rules and guarantees, date-specific hotel totals including mandatory fees, and route times for events actually selected.

Open gaps are surfaced rather than completed with assumptions.
