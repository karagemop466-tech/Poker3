Key findings

  1. Upcoming single-entry events are real and well sourced. The Venetian's DeepStack Extravaganza IV (Nov 16–Dec 23), Wynn's WPT World Championship (Nov 27–Dec 21), and Wynn's Signature Series (Jan 11–24, 2027) are confirmed on official schedules. The Venetian's DeepStack Showdown (Jan 11–Feb 14) is partly captured.
  2. The 8 AM start is not possible on verified schedules. The earliest start is 10:10 AM. Most events start at 11:00 AM or later.
  3. Multi-entry events are separated. Bellagio's weekly events allow re-entry and gratuity-for-chips. They are in Excluded.
  4. Hotel cost cannot be ranked yet. Nightly rates and walking times are unverified. Only Wynn's resort fee is confirmed ($55 per night plus tax on two terms pages; $50 on a third, flagged).
  5. Many flags remain. Bellagio stale dates, Bellagio Saturday buy-in conflict, Wynn fee conflict, Wynn Ladies NLH conflict, WPT Nevada guarantee, and missing Venetian dates. See Flagged for Review.
