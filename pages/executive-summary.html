1. Best tournament opportunities (single-entry focus)
  • Lowest cost to enter with a real guarantee: Venetian DSE IV one-day events at $200 — RaceDay Stack Nov 19–21 (10:10 AM), $200 NLH Nov 22, $200 PKO Nov 23 (5:10 PM), $200 bounty Nov 24 (5:10 PM), plus daily $200 events through Nov 30. Buy-in $200–$400, with $75K GTD multi-flight events at $300/$400 if you want bigger fields.
  • Best value daily: Wynn $200 at 12:00 PM and $160 at 6:00 PM, $10,000 GTD each (Nov 16–26, before the WPT festival takes over).
  • Biggest prize pools: WPT World Championship festival at Wynn — $400 Super Gold Million ($3M GTD, Dec 10–14), $1,100 WPT Prime ($5M GTD, Dec 5–14), $800 NLH ($1M GTD, Nov 30–Dec 4), $10,400 Championship ($1M added, Dec 13–20). These are multi-flight, so they are NOT strictly single-entry.
  • Most playing opportunities per day: Horseshoe — six daily tournaments 9:00 AM–11:00 PM ($100–$160), but re-entry is listed, so they belong in the separate section until single-entry is confirmed.
2. Hotel recommendation framework
  • Cost & quality first: Venetian, Wynn, ARIA, Bellagio are all luxury properties on site at their own tournament rooms — zero walk time. Mandatory resort fee at all of them is $55/night before tax ($62.36 with 13.38% Clark County tax). Wynn also shows some offers at $45; Encore shows conflicting $45/$55.
  • True cost per night: cannot be computed yet — base room rates for Nov–Dec 2026 are not published and are left blank. Dated samples (checked Nov 3, 2026): ARIA advance-purchase $137 all-in ($82 + $55 + tax); Bellagio advance-purchase $185 all-in ($130 + $55 + tax); Venetian from $129 + $55 fee.
  • Best on-site option: stay at the Venetian if you qualify for the poker suite rate ($119/$209 + $55 fee, code EPOKR25, requires 6 hours cash play or a $300+ tournament buy-in per night) — you're literally at the tournament room.
  • Walkable alternates: Vdara (~3 min to ARIA), Cosmopolitan (~5–7 min to Bellagio), Caesars Palace (~4 min to Bellagio), Paris (~7–10 min to Bellagio/Horseshoe) — all $55 resort fees, all ≤ 30 minutes' walk from the main venues.

4. Key risks / open items (decision blockers)
  • Single-entry status is unverified for every event. Most Las Vegas daily tournaments allow re-entry; the main table assumes single-entry only until a poker room confirms in writing.
  • Conflicting buy-ins: ARIA daily reported as $140 / $200 / $240 across sources; Horseshoe reported as $75 / $100 / $160; Wynn/Encore resort fee $45 vs $55; ARIA resort fee $50 vs $55.
  • Bellagio tournament status uncertain — multiple sources contradict (daily $150/$200 vs. tournaments suspended during WPT Five Diamond, late Nov–mid Dec).
  • Future room rates unavailable — true cost per night cannot be calculated until base rates are published/quoted.
Recommendation: Base yourself at the Venetian (poker suite rate if you qualify) or Wynn/ARIA for zero commute. Target Venetian DSE IV $200 one-day events and Wynn $160/$200 dailies as your core single-entry candidates, use the WPT festival ($400–$1,100) for bigger fields knowing they are multi-flight, and treat Horseshoe's six dailies as high-volume but non-single-entry. Confirm entry policies, final schedules, and room rates by phone before booking — nothing on this site is inferred or guaranteed.
