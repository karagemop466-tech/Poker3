How true cost per night is calculated

True cost/night = (base room rate + mandatory resort fee) × 1.1338 (Clark County lodging tax). Example for illustration only (not a quote for your dates): a $150 base rate + $55 fee = $205; $205 × 1.1338 = $232.43 per night all-in. Because base rates for Nov–Dec 2026 are blank/unverified, true cost is blank on every row — fill it in once you have a quoted base rate.
