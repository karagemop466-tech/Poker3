Suggestions for discovering the missing information

  • Call the poker rooms directly: Venetian Poker Room and Wynn Poker Room — ask about re-entry policy on each 1Day event, cash game hours from 8 AM, and confirmation of resort fees/parking for your dates.
  • Monitor official booking pages: venetianlasvegas.com/book and wynnlasvegas.com — room rates for Nov–Dec 2026 will appear within booking windows; Venetian poker suite rates only 150 days out (code EPOKR25).
  • Use walking-directions tools: measure hotel-to-poker-room walks from the published addresses (Venetian 3355 S Las Vegas Blvd; Wynn 3131 S Las Vegas Blvd) to confirm the 30-minute rule.
  • Contact Aria poker room: aria.com — request the Aria Poker Open event list with re-entry policy if you want Strip alternatives beyond Venetian/Wynn.
  • Re-verify before travel: management may cancel or change tournaments; re-check the official schedule a few days before departure.
  • Track answers here: as each question is answered, update the relevant table row and move the item from "questions" to "verified findings."
